## The organization

- MSU Entrepreneurship Association (MSUEA), a registered student org
  at Michigan State University. Founded 2006.
- Student led. Open to every major and experience level, from the
  curious first-timer to students already running a business.
- Purpose: connect students with entrepreneurship through workshops,
  speaker events, networking nights, pitch competitions, and trips.
- Goal is to inspire, educate, and create valuable experiences for the
  community. Entrepreneurship is broad by nature and the club reflects
  that.
- Connects students directly with founders, mentors, investors, and
  startup resources through the Burgess Institute for Entrepreneurship
  & Innovation.
- Part of MSU's nationally recognized entrepreneurship ecosystem,
  consistently ranked among the nation's top undergraduate
  entrepreneurship programs. One of the Burgess Institute's flagship
  student organizations.
- Community events include speaker series, experiential trips,
  hangouts, and more. Cadence: come once a month or every week.
